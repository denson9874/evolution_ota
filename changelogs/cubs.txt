Notes:
==============================
- Untested: this build has never been booted on a Pixel 11. If it doesn't boot, the phone goes back to the build you had.
- The update includes the stock Pixel firmware CD1A.261005.003 (bootloader and radio).
- Rooted? Before rebooting, install Magisk (or KernelSU) to the inactive slot, or root is gone after the update.
- Kernel: Espada v3 (Linux 6.12.92) by Kevtrejo37 (CASS scheduler, Simple LMK, GrapheneOS zram fix; source: https://github.com/atrejokm301/espada-kernel).
- First installs need a computer: see https://xdaforums.com/t/4804723/

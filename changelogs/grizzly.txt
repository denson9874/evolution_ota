Notes:
==============================

Community Fixes & Enhancements:
  - [Bug] Flashing stuck at `< waiting for any device >` after rebooting into fastbootd: recovery/fastbootd run from our vendor_boot and had no ro.recovery.usb.* IDs (stock sets 18D1/D001/4EE0 in vendor/build.prop). Added on all four phones. Also check the fastboot driver / USB hub (Reported by XDA: xerel89 (Post #22))
  - [Bug] Face unlock setup crashed Settings: the face enroll hand-off target com.google.android.settings.future.biometrics.faceenroll was missing (ActivityNotFoundException). Stock SettingsGoogleFutureFaceEnroll (system_ext priv-app, platform re-signed) added on all four phones (kodiak 9f35e0c, grizzly 7aa3484, cubs 6828376, yogi 3dbf16e). Enrollment itself not yet confirmed on a Pixel 11 (Reported by TG: Ashray V K (logs), Igor)
  - [Bug] Micro-stutter while the Dynamic Bar island shows: its backdrop blur put a blur region in the status bar window, so SurfaceFlinger GPU-composited every app frame. frameworks_base 0010 draws the island without backdrop blur (cheetah 0/1190, husky 0/937 frames client-composited). Shipped in Evolution husky 20261023 (Reported by Daryl (cheetah), wife (husky))
  - [Bug] Locked apps showed their content in Recents: Pixel Launcher doesn't know the sandbox App Lock and RecentTasks dropped isTopAppLocked's result. frameworks_base 0009 withholds snapshots of App-Locked apps, so Recents shows the lock placeholder (also while the app is unlocked). Shipped in Evolution husky 20261023. Replied to Ashray_Vk on XDA 2026-10-23 (post 90788959): fix explained, coming in the next P11 build (Reported by Daryl, XDA: Ashray_Vk (Post #90787566))
  - [Bug] Settings > Connected devices > NFC > Contactless payments (and Wallet's default-payment link) opened a broken page: PaymentSettingsActivity pointed at the removed nfc.PaymentSettings fragment. Evolution X Settings 8b2dded5b421 (ExtremeXT) carried as patches/packages_apps_Settings/0001 (Reported by Release review (critic))
